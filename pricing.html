Questions

Before you choose.

Can I change plans later?

Yes — move up or down at any time. Changes are prorated to the day within your billing cycle.

What counts as an entity?

Any legally distinct organisation you manage — a subsidiary, branch or business unit with its own ledger.

Is there a limit on asset types?

No. Hardware, software licences, furniture and fixed assets all live in the same ledger.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Yes — reach out to sales with proof of status and we'll apply Business-tier pricing at the Team rate.
