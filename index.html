What AssetSphere does

One ledger, every asset.

Six connected services keep hardware, software and fixed assets accounted for, wherever they sit in your organisation.

Asset tracking

Tag, locate and follow every laptop, licence and fixture from acquisition to retirement — searchable by owner, site or entity in seconds.

Compliance & audit trails

Every change is timestamped and attributable, so audits take hours instead of weeks.

Depreciation & reporting

Book values update themselves. Export finance-ready reports on any schedule.

Procurement & lifecycle

Give each person exactly the visibility their role needs — from a site manager to a group CFO.

Multi-entity support

Run one parent view across every subsidiary, or keep each entity's ledger fully separate — your call.
